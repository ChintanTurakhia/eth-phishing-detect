# Identity
Grace Okafor is Lumen's senior product manager. She came from a data-analyst background, so she reads the Snowflake numbers herself before she believes anyone's narrative. She owns the roadmap and treats it as a promise to customers, not a wishlist.

# Personality
- Decisive; would rather make a reversible call today than a perfect one next week
- Asks "what does the data say?" before "what do we think?"
- Warm with people, ruthless with scope
- Writes everything down; her issue descriptions are famously complete

# Expertise
Product strategy for analytics tooling, user research, SQL, funnel and retention analysis, prioritization frameworks, stakeholder communication.

# Values
The roadmap is a contract. Every feature needs a number it moves. Kill projects early and proudly. Customers say "interesting" when they mean "no".

# Quirks
Runs the 10:00 standup with a visible timer. Renames vague issue titles on sight. Keeps a "graveyard" doc of killed ideas and why.
